Introduction

Indoor Cricket Simulator is a single-player mobile management game built using Unity, in which you run your own indoor cricket club. The game does not require account creation, login, or any personal details to play.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the app.

Information Collection

Indoor Cricket Simulator does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the app uses a third-party advertising service that may automatically collect certain information in order to show ads.

Types of Data Collected

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App interaction data needed to deliver and measure ads (for example, ads viewed or tapped)
  • Advertising identifiers (where permitted by the user)
  • General location data (country or region level, derived from your IP address)
  • Diagnostic information used by the advertising service to keep ads working and to prevent fraud

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress (such as your venues, money, built pitches, staff, upgrades, and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not linked to any personal identity. It is not sent to the developer.

Purpose of Data Usage

  • Display advertisements (personalized or non-personalized depending on user consent)
  • Measure ad performance and prevent advertising fraud
  • Keep the app and its ads working correctly

Advertising

Indoor Cricket Simulator uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on your location and consent choice:

  • Personalized ads may be shown based on advertising identifiers and user interests.
  • Non-personalized ads may be shown based on contextual information such as app content or general location.

Users in regions where required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and certain US states) are presented with a consent or privacy choice message. Users may modify their preferences at any time via the in-app privacy settings.

Consent Management

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage user consent where legally required.

Users may accept, decline, or modify advertising preferences at any time using the privacy settings available within the app.

Third-Party Services

Indoor Cricket Simulator uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob (advertising services)

This service may collect and process information, such as your IP address, according to its own privacy policy. For more information, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, or rent user data. Data is shared only with the third-party service listed above for advertising functionality.

Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not store any of your data on its own servers. Advertising data is kept by the third-party service above according to its own retention settings and policies.

You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by emailing the developer at mhassan.tariq1998.dev@gmail.com. Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress.

Children’s Privacy

Indoor Cricket Simulator is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Where required by law, personalized advertising is disabled for users identified as children, and non-personalized ads are shown instead.

Regional Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, to restrict its processing, or to opt out of personalized advertising.

These preferences can be managed through the in-app privacy settings or by contacting the developer.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by third-party services from unauthorized access or misuse.
