Data Deletion Request

App Name: Indoor Cricket Simulator

Developer: Hassan Tariq

Indoor Cricket Simulator does not require user accounts or any personal details, and the developer does not store any of your data on its own servers. The advertising service used in the app may collect limited data automatically.

What data may exist

  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): device information, advertising identifier where permitted, general location (country or region) and ad interaction data

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

How to request data deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your use of Indoor Cricket Simulator, please contact the developer at:
Email: mhassan.tariq1998.dev@gmail.com

In your request, please include:

  • The name of the app (Indoor Cricket Simulator)
  • Your device model and the approximate date you installed the app

Requests will be processed as soon as possible. Data handled by third-party services will be deleted according to their respective policies.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device’s settings, and change your ad consent choice in the game’s privacy settings.
