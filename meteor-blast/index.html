Introduction

Meteor Blast is a single-player mobile arcade game built using Unity. The game does not require account creation, login, or any personal details to play.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the app.

Information Collection

Meteor Blast does not contain advertisements and does not use analytics or advertising services.

The only information that leaves your device is what the online leaderboard needs.

Online Leaderboard

To place you on the global leaderboard, the game signs in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or Google account. The leaderboard then stores, under that identifier:

  • Your player name (a randomly generated callsign, which you may change in the game)
  • Your best score and best distance
  • Your rank on the in-game ladder
  • The time of your last update

Your player name, scores, and rank are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your player name.

As with any online service, Google Firebase may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver this service, according to Google's privacy policy.

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress (such as your best score, experience points, unlocked fire skins, and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not linked to any personal identity.

Purpose of Data Usage

  • Show your best score and distance on the global leaderboard
  • Show your rank among other players

Third-Party Services

Meteor Blast uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Firebase (anonymous authentication and leaderboard storage)

This service processes information according to its own privacy policy. For more information, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, or rent user data. Leaderboard data is processed by Google Firebase only to provide the leaderboard.

Data Retention and Deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is active. You can ask for your leaderboard entry to be deleted at any time by emailing the developer at mhassan.tariq1998.dev@gmail.com with your player name. Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress.

Children’s Privacy

Meteor Blast is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Regional Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. To exercise these rights, please contact the developer.

Data Security

Data sent to the leaderboard is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and leaderboard rules only allow each player to update their own entry.
