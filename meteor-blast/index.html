Introduction

Meteor Blast is a single-player mobile arcade game built using Unity. The game does not require account creation, login, or any personal details to play.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the app.

Information Collection

Meteor Blast does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the app uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information for the online leaderboard, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising.

Types of Data Collected

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App usage data (session duration, levels played, feature interactions)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics
  • Advertising identifiers (where permitted by the user)
  • General location data (country or region level)
  • Leaderboard data (described below)

Online Leaderboard

To place you on the global leaderboard, the game signs in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or Google account. The leaderboard then stores, under that identifier:

  • Your player name (a randomly generated callsign, which you may change in the game)
  • Your best score and best distance
  • Your rank on the in-game ladder
  • The time of your last update

Your player name, scores, and rank are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your player name.

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress (such as your scores, levels, stars, upgrades, skins, and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not linked to any personal identity.

Purpose of Data Usage

  • Show your best score, distance, and rank on the global leaderboard
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Analyze gameplay to improve the game, for example level difficulty and length
  • Detect and fix crashes and technical issues
  • Display advertisements (personalized or non-personalized depending on user consent)

Advertising

Meteor Blast uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on your location and consent choice:

  • Personalized ads may be shown based on advertising identifiers and user interests.
  • Non-personalized ads may be shown based on contextual information such as app content or general location.

Users in regions where required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and certain US states) are presented with a consent or privacy choice message. Users may modify their preferences at any time via the in-app privacy settings.

Consent Management

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage user consent where legally required.

Users may accept, decline, or modify advertising preferences at any time using the privacy settings available within the app.

Third-Party Services

Meteor Blast uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (anonymous sign-in and leaderboard storage)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)
  • Google AdMob (advertising services)

These services may collect and process information, such as your IP address, according to their own privacy policies. For more information, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, trade, or rent user data. Data is shared only with the third-party services listed above for the leaderboard, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising functionality.

Data Retention and Deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is active. Analytics, crash, and advertising data are kept by the third-party services above according to their own retention settings and policies.

You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by emailing the developer at mhassan.tariq1998.dev@gmail.com (see the data deletion page). Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress.

Children’s Privacy

Meteor Blast is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Where required by law, personalized advertising is disabled for users identified as children, and non-personalized ads are shown instead.

Regional Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, to restrict its processing, or to opt out of personalized advertising.

These preferences can be managed through the in-app privacy settings or by contacting the developer.

Data Security

Data sent to the leaderboard is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and leaderboard rules only allow each player to update their own entry. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by third-party services from unauthorized access or misuse.
