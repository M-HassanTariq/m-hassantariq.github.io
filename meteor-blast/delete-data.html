Data Deletion Request

App Name: Meteor Blast

Developer: Hassan Tariq

Meteor Blast does not require user accounts or any personal details. The only data stored online is your entry on the global leaderboard.

What data may exist

  • A random anonymous identifier (created by Google Firebase Authentication)
  • Your player name, best score, best distance, and ladder rank on the leaderboard

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

How to request data deletion

To have your leaderboard entry deleted, please contact the developer at:
Email: mhassan.tariq1998.dev@gmail.com

In your request, please include:

  • The name of the app (Meteor Blast)
  • Your player name as shown on the leaderboard
  • Your best score (if you remember it)

Requests will be processed as soon as possible, and your leaderboard entry and its identifier will be permanently removed.
