Data Deletion Request

App Name: Meteor Blast

Developer: Hassan Tariq

Meteor Blast does not require user accounts or any personal details. Some data is stored online for the global leaderboard, and third-party services used in the app may collect limited data automatically.

What data may exist

  • A random anonymous identifier (created by Google Firebase Authentication)
  • Your player name, best score, best distance, and ladder rank on the leaderboard
  • App usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

How to request data deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your use of Meteor Blast, please contact the developer at:
Email: mhassan.tariq1998.dev@gmail.com

In your request, please include:

  • The name of the app (Meteor Blast)
  • Your player name as shown on the leaderboard
  • Your best score (if you remember it)
  • Your device model and the approximate date you installed the app

Requests will be processed as soon as possible. Your leaderboard entry and its identifier will be permanently removed. Data handled by third-party services will be deleted according to their respective policies.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device’s settings, and change your ad consent choice in the game’s privacy settings.
